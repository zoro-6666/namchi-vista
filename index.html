A considered place to live

Make room for everyday life.

Namchi Vista is a residential apartment building in Kamrang, South Sikkim, with four residential floors and two homes on each floor around a central staircase.

Choose from thoughtfully planned 1, 2 and 3 BHK homes. Each home has a balcony, with living areas, bedrooms and practical details designed for day-to-day comfort.
